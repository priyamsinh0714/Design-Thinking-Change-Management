# Design Thinking and Change Management Plan  

## Overview  
This project applies design thinking principles to create a change management plan for addressing a socio-economic challenge. It emphasizes human-centred approaches, integrating data-driven solutions (e.g., advanced analytics, AI) with conventional strategies to tackle complex real-world problems.  

---

## Objectives  
1. Identify a specific socio-economic challenge and its stakeholder impacts.  
2. Develop a change management plan that combines data-intensive and non-data-intensive solutions.  
3. Use techniques like Empath Map and Lotus Blossom for stakeholder analysis and idea generation.  
4. Deliver a professional report and presentation, summarizing findings and recommendations.

### 2. Design Thinking Techniques  
- **Empath Map:** Understanding stakeholders’ problems, emotions, and perspectives.  
- **Lotus Blossom:** Generating and exploring potential solutions for identified problems.  

### 3. Solution Development  
- **Data-Driven Solutions:**  
  - Big data analytics for skill gap identification.  
  - AI-driven matching systems for employers and job seekers.  
  - Predictive modeling to forecast future skill demands.  
- **Conventional Solutions:**  
  - Career counseling and upskilling programs.  
  - Policy recommendations for educational institutions and governments.
